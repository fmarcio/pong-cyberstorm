GAME RULES & CODEX

Complete guide to court mechanics, active spells, collectible items, and tactical AI.

Controls & Energy System

• Player 1: Mouse (fluid tracking) or Keyboard W / S.

• Player 2 (PvP Mode): Arrow Keys ↑ / ↓.

• Pause / Resume: Press SPACE or P at any time.

• Energy Bar: Recharges passively (+8/sec) and gains +15 energy on each paddle deflection.

Active Spells & Powers (Cast via HUD or Hotkeys)

• Fireball (1 / Q - 35 Energy): Launches at 2x velocity straight towards the opponent's side.

• Freeze (2 / E - 45 Energy): Glaciates opponent paddle, slashing their speed by 50% for 4s.

• Multiball (3 / R - 50 Energy): Clones active balls, multiplying the chaos on the table.

• Force Shield (4 / F - 40 Energy): Deploys a laser barrier behind your paddle that deflects any missed shot.

In-Court Items (Stackable with 5s Timers)

• Reduce (Crimson): -30% opponent paddle size for 5s.

• Size it up (Emerald): +30% your paddle size for 5s.

• Slow down (Cyan): -30% ball speed for 5s.

• Energy boost (Gold): +30% energy instantly.

• Energy down (Purple): -30% opponent energy.

• Bomb (Red-Orange): Rare hazard! Ball inflates for 5s and explodes into shrapnel mini-balls. Every undefended shrapnel awards +1 point to the opponent.

• Invisibility (Indigo): Ball flickers between standard and 20% opacity while travelling towards the opponent for 5s.

(All effects stack cumulatively with independent countdown timers!)

Tactical AI Opponent

Calculates real-time bounce trajectories, builds energy, and unleashes spells against you!

Available Difficulties: Easy (relaxed reaction), Medium (balanced calculation), and Insane (instant predictive intercepts).
